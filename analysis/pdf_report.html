Key findings

  • The network is healthy and stable. Transaction errors hold a flat 6–9% school-day baseline for the whole quarter with no drift; ~30% of transactions process offline by design and reconcile later. The 18–25% error spikes are confined to low-volume weekend event days.
  • But a small set of hardware needs attention: 12 terminals run 19–25% error rates (TKGS, ACSI, BESS…), and 45 WiFi-connected terminals report signal in dBm — corrupting signal analytics until units are normalised.
  • Speed is flat, not degrading — except one stack. Median transaction time stays ~2.3s across all three months. The CONCESSION/EZLINK "3.0 ASCAN" readers, however, run ~2× slower (4.5s median) than the 2.0 stack — every week, at every school where they appear.
  • Where stall names are trustworthy (498 stalls, 17.2% of purchases), the menu is three-point: the $1 drink, the $1–2 snack, and the $2 cooked meal — and that mix is stable week to week. The other 2,130 stalls give no trustworthy category (generic "Stall 1" or bare company names); per service-team policy they are reported as Unclassified (price facts only), never guessed.
  • "Atas" schools do spend more. Among MOE-matched schools, Independent-school students average $3.84 per active day ($2.70 ticket) vs $3.41 ($1.93) at government schools — driven by pricier purchases, not more of them. Level matters more than status: secondary students out-spend primary by 40% regardless of school type.
  • Same-level schools still differ up to 3× in spend per student-day ($2 → $6.8) — split between "expensive ticket" schools (bookshop-heavy) and "high frequency" schools.
Method note: food categories are stated only for stalls whose registered names say what they sell; generic-named stalls are Unclassified — no item-level SKU data exists and we do not guess. School identities/types come from acronym-matching IDs to the MOE directory — 158 of 334 matched confidently; unmatched schools are included in every metric but carry no map position or MOE type. All inference is flagged where used.

Appendix · Method & caveats

Data preparation

  • 13,402,006 raw transactions; excluded from analysis: vendor test terminals (school code "STYL", 1-cent night traffic), "Retrieve…" card-maintenance flows, and amounts >$200 (maintenance/write-off artefacts). Amounts are cents SGD (stakeholder-confirmed).
  • Latency uses startToFinish (ms); component timings (checkSignal / openSocket / handshake / transfer) from the reader telemetry. 219k rows with missing/zero duration excluded from speed stats.
  • Signal analytics use the 0–31 cellular scale only; WiFi dBm readings are reported separately.

Food classification policy

  • No item/SKU column exists. A stall is categorised only if its registered name says what it sells (keyword match). 498 stalls qualify — 17.2% of purchase volume. A category is kept only when the stall's own prices don't contradict it (a “cooked food” name trading at a $0.60 modal is not counted as cooked food); names covering two food groups (“Hot Drinks and Snacks”) count as Mixed; bare company names (“BL Marketing Pte Ltd”) are Unclassified until the vendor list confirms them. Everything else is Unclassified: reported by factual price statistics, never assigned a food type.
  • An earlier price-profile clustering step (inferring categories from price patterns) was removed at the service team's request — an estimate, however plausible, must not read as fact. Because named stalls are not a random sample (bookshops nearly always carry real names), verified shares describe named stalls only.

School identity & "atas" classification

  • School IDs were acronym-matched to the MOE School Directory (data.gov.sg), including pinyin-syllable and CHIJ rules; ambiguous IDs were left unmatched rather than guessed. 158/334 matched (4 hand-verified: HCI ×2, RI, RGS). Coordinates via OneMap postal-code geocoding.
  • School type (Government / Government-aided / Independent), IP, GEP, SAP flags come from MOE data for matched schools only. Level for unmatched schools is inferred from ID suffixes (…PS/…P primary; …SS/…GS/…HS secondary; …JC junior college).
  • Per-card stats cover the 73% of transactions carrying a cardId (bank-card taps are anonymous).
