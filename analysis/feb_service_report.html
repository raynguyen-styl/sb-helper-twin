    Appendix · Method & caveats

    Data preparation

    • 3.96M February transactions after excluding vendor test terminals (school code "STYL"), "Retrieve…" card-maintenance flows, and amounts >$200. Amounts are cents SGD (stakeholder-confirmed).
    • Speed uses startToFinish (ms) from reader telemetry; component timings (checkSignal / openSocket / handshake / transfer) are medians over online transactions. Zero/negative durations excluded.
    • 4G signal analytics use the 0–31 cellular scale; WiFi terminals report RSSI in dBm in the same field — reported separately, not mixed in.

    Machine-vs-location verdict

    • A terminal is flagged bad if its error rate ≥10% (with ≥300 txns) or its median transaction ≥4 s. It is then compared to its school peers (other terminals, ≥300 txns, ≥2 peers): healthy peers ⇒ machine fault; peers also bad (peer-median error ≥8% or ≥4 s) ⇒ site issue; no peers to compare ⇒ check.
    • The interactive app's Terminal Health window lets the team browse every unit and its verdict, with per-day error/speed vs the school baseline.

    Food classification policy & school inference

    • No item/SKU column exists. A stall gets a food category only if its registered name says what it sells (keyword match: "…Drinks…", "…Bakery…", "…Noodle…"). 493 stalls qualify (17.5% of purchases). A category is kept only when the stall's own prices don't contradict it (a “cooked food” name trading at a $0.60 modal is not counted as cooked food); names covering two food groups (“Hot Drinks and Snacks”) count as Mixed; bare company names (“BL Marketing Pte Ltd”) are Unclassified until the vendor list confirms them. The remaining 2,053 stalls are Unclassified — reported by factual price bands, never assigned a food type. An earlier price-profile clustering step (guessing categories from price patterns) has been removed at the service team's request.
    • Because bookshops and drink stalls register descriptive names more often than generic canteen stalls, the verified sample is not a random sample of the network — its category shares describe named stalls only.
    • School IDs acronym-matched to the MOE School Directory (data.gov.sg). School type (Government / Government-aided / Independent) is available for matched schools only; level for the rest is inferred from ID suffixes. "Spend per student-day" covers the transactions carrying a cardId (bank-card taps are anonymous).

    Chinese New Year

    • CNY window = 16–18 Feb; 17–18 Feb are public holidays with $0 canteen revenue. Every spending/food figure includes the whole month, with the CNY effect flagged rather than removed — the "school-day" mix on page 4 already excludes weekends and CNY for the clean term-time picture.
